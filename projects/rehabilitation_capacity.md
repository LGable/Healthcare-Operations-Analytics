## Separate scenario: 2023 facility patient-stay revenue

30 short-term turnovers/month; specified payer shares of 40% Medicare, 30% Medicaid, 20% private and **10% unclassified**. Illustrative daily rates from the prior modeling discussion: Medicare $552.21, Medicaid $241.80 and private $370.74. Rates vary by facility, case-mix and contract; verify original dated sources before external publication.

Weighted rate for classified payer volume = 0.40(552.21)+0.30(241.80)+0.20(370.74) = **$367.572 per total discharged case-day**, excluding 10% unclassified.

At 15 days of stay, estimated modeled monthly gross facility patient-stay revenue = 30 × 15 × $367.572 = **$165,407.40**; annual = **$1,984,888.80**. Actual model values may differ from a prior workbook based on rounding or input assumptions: reconcile before publishing.
