# Revenue Cycle & Cash-Flow Stability | Economic Impact Model

**Type:** Retrospective illustrative financial scenario inspired by self-reported experience, NOT an employer financial statement.

## Business context

A behavioral-health organization faces claims delays, periodic payroll-timing difficulties, and administrative burden. Leadership needs clarity on overdue receivables, staffing commitments and repeatable reporting.

## Owner-reported inputs to validate

| Input | Estimate | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Organizational payroll | $69,000/month | Exposure, not controlled budget |
| Receivables collected after follow-up | $2,000/month | Cash recovery; not necessarily new revenue |
| Avoided fees and interest | $300/month | Potential direct cost avoidance |
| Administrative labor capacity released | 20 hours/month | Opportunity cost at $30 loaded rate |
| Power BI reporting efficiency | 20 min/report, 5 reports/week | Assume overlapping with 20 hours/month until proven separate |

## Correct annualization

- Payroll exposure: **$828,000** (= $69,000 × 12).
- Recovered receivables: **$24,000** (= $2,000 × 12), annualized; not booked as new profit.
- Potential fees avoided: **$3,600** (= $300 × 12), pending confirmation with actual charges.
- Labor-capacity value: **$7,200** (= 20 × $30 × 12), not necessarily reduced payroll.
- Preliminary combined economic value: **$10,800** (= $3,600 + $7,200), excluding the $24,000 recovered receivables.
- Do **not** add the standalone Power BI $2,600 capacity estimate to HBH $7,200 without verifying nonoverlap.

## Analysis and controls

Segregate cash collections from revenue recognition, identify duplicate claims, validate paid dates, measure fees before and after, monitor documentation rework time, and define accountable operational owners. Evaluate whether owner cash infusions decreased financing expense, but never count principal transfers as savings.

## Portfolio ethics

All numbers above are owner-supplied estimates, not audited figures. The linked dataset is generated for illustration. Reproduce the model using a fictional scenario if publicly presenting charts. No real payer, payroll or patient records are included.
